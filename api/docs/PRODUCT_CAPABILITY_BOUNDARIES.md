# Product capability boundaries

## What may be claimed today

- Integrated CRM, customer portal, LMS, HR/attendance, support and operational
  accounting for the institute.
- Double-entry journals for implemented payment/refund/expense flows.
- Controlled multi-tenant pilot with scoped identities, data and configuration.

## What must not be claimed yet

- “Enterprise SaaS ready” until metering, institute billing/dunning, managed
  production infrastructure, residency evidence and tenant restore drills pass.
- “Full ERP” until accounts payable/receivable subledgers, vendor/customer
  statements, fixed assets/depreciation, tax engine/returns, purchasing/inventory
  and independently validated period close are implemented.
- “Paymob ready” while `PAYMOB_REVIEW_PENDING=true`.

Readiness percentages inside setup screens describe configuration completeness,
not commercial or production certification.
